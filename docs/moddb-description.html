A moving sea for journeys across Earth.

Sea was developed primarily for Earth: to give its oceans travelling waves, rough storm water, believable reflections and boats that move with the surface. Earth provides the climate, weather and changing winds; Sea turns those winds into the water you sail on. Sea also works without Earth.

Current release: 0.6.3. Without Earth, wind direction mods (Pelagus Winds, Wind Vanes) now steer ships' and sailboats' sails, and a server admin can turn the vanilla sailboat's wind sailing off with SailboatsByWind. Coasts (surf that breaks on beaches, the lee of islands and headlands, waves thrown back by cliffs, longshore currents and rips) are off by default since 0.6.2 until they are reworked; a server admin can switch them on in sea.json. Storm seas are steeper and throw spray off their crests. Full documentation is in the wiki.

Waves and storm seas

  • Waves follow the wind: the sea builds over hours of wind and settles gradually afterwards. Long swell and short chop move together, rather than repeating the same bumps everywhere.
  • Sharper crests and broader troughs: rough water has peaked crests, with foam where crests break and, from a fresh breeze on, spray torn off them.
  • Storms: a storm sea is about 2.5 blocks high and a hurricane's over 5. An admin can make storm seas higher per world.
  • One shared sea: the server and every player work out the same waves, so boats and ship mods ride the same surface that players see.
  • The sea, not the rivers: Sea's waves run on water the world's ocean map counts as sea and fade in through bays and estuaries; rivers and lakes keep vanilla's ripples.

Coasts (off by default since 0.6.2)

Set "Coasts": true in the server's sea.json to try them; they are being reworked.

  • Surf: over shallow water the swell turns toward the beach, steepens and breaks, with whitewater and spray. Crests reach a beach one stretch after another.
  • Shelter: islands and headlands keep waves off the water behind them.
  • Reflection: cliffs and sea walls throw waves back, so the water before them stands up and down.
  • Currents and rips: waves breaking at an angle drive water along the beach and back out through rip channels; rafts and sailboats drift with it.
  • The coast follows the blocks: a channel you dig or a sea wall you build changes the surf.

Vanilla rafts and sailboats

Sea makes the vanilla raft and sailboat rise, pitch and roll with the waves, including their seats and interaction targets. These features work with Sea alone; Ships is not required.

With Sea installed, the vanilla sailboat is driven by wind direction and strength. Its yard and sail turn with the wind by themselves, and it can make progress on either tack from about 45° off the wind. Sailing directly into the wind does not work. The normal furled, half-sail and full-sail controls remain; furl the sail to row. With Earth the sail follows Earth's wind; without a weather mod it takes the game's own wind: its strength from the west, or the direction a wind direction mod such as Pelagus Winds or Wind Vanes gives it. To sail without the wind, set SailboatsByWind to false in sea.json (Ships has SailByWind in ships.json).

Storms can swamp the vanilla sailboat. Water coming over the gunwale collects aboard, reduces buoyancy and can eventually sink it. This happens whether the boat is occupied or unattended. Water aboard is saved with the boat, and its level is visible inside the hull.

To bail: sneak and right-click the hull with an empty bucket. Each use throws water overboard, with a short cooldown; the bucket remains empty. The boat's information text shows how much water is aboard. Bail it dry before picking it up.

Underwater effects and breathing for a rider follow the real waves, so riding in a trough does not by itself put a dry rider underwater. A crest that actually covers you, or floodwater inside the boat, still counts as water.

Water, light and larger ships

Sea adds sky reflections, sunlight glitter and moonlight trails shaped by the waves, plus adjustable outdoor night visibility. Optional integration with Astra Terra for Earth and World Map (0.5 or later) helps the near water, sky and distant sea agree. With SheyderMod, Sea's water code goes into its water shader and Sea keeps the water's reflections.

Ships uses Sea's waves and keeps the water out of its hulls through Sea. Larger ships keep their own sailing, damage and flooding mechanics.

Earth and Rough Seas

Earth owns the climate, wind and weather; Sea supplies the waves and the water's look. Following discussions with hoblin, Sea and Rough Seas stay separate mods. Sea publishes its wind, waves and hull water cuts in an open form that any mod can read without depending on Sea; Rough Seas doesn't use it so far. Running Sea and Rough Seas together is not supported.

Installation

For Vintage Story 1.22. Install the same Sea version on the server and every client. In singleplayer, put the ZIP in your Mods folder without extracting it. Fully close the game before replacing an older Sea package.

Earth is optional for Sea, and Sea is strongly recommended but optional for Earth. With Earth installed, the sea follows Earth's changing winds and storms. Without a weather mod, vanilla weather continues normally and the waves follow a configurable steady wind. An administrator can also set a fixed wind for testing.

Sea does not add an Earth play style or generate terrain. To create an Earth world, follow Earth's installation instructions.

Settings and commands

Storm wave height follows StormHeightScale in the server's sea.json; an admin can set it per world with /worldconfig seaStormHeight 1.5 (json, 1, 1.5 or 2.2; restart to apply). Other settings are in VintagestoryData/ModConfig/sea.json: overall wave scale, the steady sea wind without a weather mod, sailboats sailing by the wind, coasts, reflections and night visibility. Every setting is explained in the wiki.

  • /sea reports the wind and waves at your location; .sea shows what Sea is doing on your game; .sea coast shows the coast where you stand.
  • .sea night, .sea sky and .sea glint, followed by a number from 0 to 2, adjust the view for the current session. Use default or .sea reset to return to the configured values.
  • .sea foam all or .sea foam off shows or hides Sea's foam and spray on your game.
  • /sea setwind <from> <strength> [hours] [built] fixes the wind for testing; /sea clearwind returns control to the weather. These commands require server-control permission.

Development and feedback

Sea is under active development. The coast and storm features were checked in game on one client; sailing balance and multiplayer combinations need more play. Other water-shader replacements can conflict with Sea.

When reporting a problem, include the game and Sea versions, other installed mods, the wind and conditions, client/server logs, and a screenshot or short reproduction. Source code and releases: GitHub.
